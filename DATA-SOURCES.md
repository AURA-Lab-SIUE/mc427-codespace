# Data sources

| File | Contents | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `nms-steam-monthly-players-2016-2026.csv` | Monthly average and peak Steam player counts for No Man's Sky | Public counts copied from SteamCharts (steamcharts.com). |
| `nms-x-monthly-2016-2021.csv` | Monthly counts and percentages of public #NoMansSky posts on X | Derived from the course's archive of public posts; no post text and no account names. |
